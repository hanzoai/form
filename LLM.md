# hanzoai/form

Forms: a form is defined once (fields, screens, hidden values, theme, sinks),
published at a public address, answered anonymously or signed in, and every
response is routed to the places the form names.

Served at `/v1/form` on api.hanzo.ai by hanzo-inc/cloud, which embeds this
module as it embeds hanzoai/iam and hanzoai/commerce.
